Hi there 👋 I'm CTO at [Warpnet](https://warpnet.nl) and [Enlite](https://enlite.nl), a software engineer based in the Netherlands working across **security**, **platform engineering**, and **AI**. Over the past decade I've worked at organizations like [DUO](https://duo.nl/), [KPN](https://www.kpn.com/), [CJIB](https://www.cjib.nl/), and [DICTU](https://www.dictu.nl/).

I've contributed to open source projects like [SaltStack](https://github.com/saltstack/salt/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aroaldnefs), [Prometheus](https://github.com/prometheus/client_golang/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aroaldnefs), and [rfcat](https://github.com/atlas0fd00m/rfcat/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Aroaldnefs), and maintain [a few of my own](https://github.com/roaldnefs), such as [WebUSB CAN](https://github.com/roaldnefs/webusb-can), the browser-based CAN console I use in my automotive workshops. Outside of work I keep ending up in automotive security and hardware hacking, and I tinker with electronics, RF, and lockpicking.

I co-organize [BSides Groningen](https://bsidesgrunn.org/), [BSides Amsterdam](https://www.bsidesams.org/), and [Cloud Native Groningen](https://cloudnativegroningen.nl/), and run [hands-on workshops](https://roaldnefs.com/training/) on automotive security and hardware hacking.

Please find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/roaldnefs/) for a more detailed description of my full work experience, education and certification, or on [Mastodon](https://infosec.exchange/@roald). You can get in touch with me by email at [info@roaldnefs.com](mailto:info@roaldnefs.com) ([PGP](https://roaldnefs.com/key.asc)).
