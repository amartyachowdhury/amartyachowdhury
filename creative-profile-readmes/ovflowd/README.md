<h3 align="center">Hi, I'm Claudio Wunder 👋</h3>

<p align="center"><samp>open source maintainer · adjunct lecturer at TUM · platform engineer at HubSpot</samp></p>

<br>

I've been doing open source for over a decade, mostly on the parts developers touch every day: websites, docs, tooling and the infrastructure behind them. These days I lead the [Node.js](https://github.com/nodejs/node) Web Infra team, the folxs behind nodejs.org, our API docs and our downloads.

Along the way I secured [partnerships](https://nodejs.org/en/about/partners) for Node.js with Vercel, Cloudflare, SpaceX (Cursor) and many other orgs, went through the [GitHub Secure Open Source Fund](https://resources.github.com/github-secure-open-source-fund/) twice (Node.js and webpack) and sat on the OpenJS Foundation's Cross Project Council. I've mentored for Google Summer of Code since 2019, with GNOME (where I'm a Foundation member) and webpack, and in 2023 the OpenJS Foundation gave me its [JavaScriptLandia Award](https://openjsf.org/blog/2023/05/10/javascriptlandia-community-awards-showcase-creativity-diversity-and-energy/).

By day I'm a platform engineer at [HubSpot](https://github.com/HubSpot), working on the frontend infrastructure hundreds of teams rely on. At TUM I teach the [Open Source Lab](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/cm/teaching/), where students spend a semester landing pull requests in real open source projects.

<summary><b>How I got here</b></summary>
<br>

I started out writing servers and CMSs for Habbo retro communities ([Chocolatey](https://github.com/ovflowd/chocolatey), [Yupi](https://github.com/ovflowd/yupi)). At the University of Brasília I joined the [UIoT lab](https://github.com/uiot), went from designing its apps to running its infrastructure team, built the [RAISe](https://github.com/ovflowd/raise) middleware and co-authored papers like _RAISe: REST API Approach for IoT Services_. Around the same time I became one of the first [GitHub Campus Experts](https://education.github.com/experts), a Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador, started Hacktoberfest Brasília and ran Git and GitHub workshops at [Campus Party Brasil](https://web.archive.org/web/20191118160030/https://campuse.ro/events/campus-party-brasil-2019/workshop/introducao-ao-git-e-github-cpbr12/).

That's also when I joined GNOME's Engagement team and spoke at GUADEC 2018 about bringing IoT to the desktop. Over the next few years I kept at it: outreach, campaigns, our websites and [Faces of GNOME](https://gitlab.gnome.org/Teams/Engagement/websites/faces-of-gnome). Along the way I mentored several students through GSoC. Then came Node.js, webpack and the OpenJS Foundation.
