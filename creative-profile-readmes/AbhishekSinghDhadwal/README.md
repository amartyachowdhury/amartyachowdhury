### 👨🏻‍💻 &nbsp;About Me

🎓 &nbsp;I'm a PhD student in Health Systems Research at the University of Toronto (IHPME), and I get to work out of the Digital Mental Health Lab at CAMH!\
🧠 &nbsp;These days I'm digging into AI safety in health care, especially how large language models behave when people turn to them for mental health support.\
🏥 &nbsp;Before Toronto, I did my MS in Computer Science (Biomedical Informatics) at Arizona State University, where I built tools that help patients control how their sensitive health data gets shared.\
💼 &nbsp;I also spent three fun years writing trading and compliance software at Credit Suisse, and graduated from VNIT Nagpur with a B.Tech in Computer Science.\
🎸 &nbsp;When I'm not at my desk, I'm practicing bass guitar (suboptimally), playing football, or hunting down the best thrift finds in Toronto. Shoutout to Kensington Market!\
💬 &nbsp;Always happy to chat about health AI, AI safety, FHIR, or any of the projects here, so feel free to reach out!\
🌐 &nbsp;You can find my publications and projects at [abhisheksinghdhadwal.github.io](https://abhisheksinghdhadwal.github.io).
